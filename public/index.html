Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026 · Effective immediately

Your trust is the entire game for us. JibuFlow only works if you feel safe handing us the keys to your comments and DMs — so here's exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it. No dark patterns, no fine‑print surprises.

1. The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run your automations.
  • We encrypt your data in transit and at rest.
  • We never sell your data. Not now, not ever.
  • We only read the comments & DMs you connect — we don't post as you without permission.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time.

2. What we collect

  • Account info: your name, email, a hashed password, your business type, the context you write about your business, and the WhatsApp number you use for lead hand‑off.
  • Connection data: the TikTok post links you add, plus the comments and DMs on those specific posts that our AI needs to read and reply to.
  • Usage data: the numbers you see on your dashboard (comments processed, replies sent, conversion stats) and basic app analytics (pages visited, errors) so we can keep the product healthy.

We do not collect your TikTok password, and we do not read posts or messages you didn't connect.

3. How we use it (and why it helps you)

  • To reply to your customers instantly and flag high‑intent buyers — that's the whole point of the product.
  • To power the dashboard stats that tell you what's actually working.
  • To improve reply quality over time and to fix bugs quickly.
  • To contact you about your account, billing, or important changes.

We don't use your private messages to advertise other products to you, and we don't sell conversation data to anyone.

4. The TikTok connection & its limits

The connection is limited and revocable. We act only on the posts you connect, we never post, follow, or change your profile without you, and the moment you disconnect we stop reading. Think of it as handing us a single key to a single room — not the whole house.

5. How we keep your data safe

  • Encryption in transit (TLS / HTTPS) so nobody can snoop on the wire between you and us.
  • Encryption at rest using industry‑standard AES‑256 for stored data.
  • Hashed passwords with strong one‑way algorithms — we literally cannot see your password.
  • Least‑privilege access: only the few engineers who need it can touch production data, and every access is logged.
  • Regular security reviews and prompt dependency updates.
  • No selling or renting of data to brokers or advertisers.

Why this matters to you: it means that even in a worst‑case scenario somewhere else on the internet, your customers' conversations and your account stay protected — and with them, your brand's reputation.

6. Cookies & analytics

We use a minimal functional cookie to keep you logged in, plus privacy‑friendly analytics to understand crashes and usage. We do not run creepy cross‑site ad tracking on you.

7. Third parties we work with

To deliver the service we work with TikTok (to read and reply on connected posts), payment processors such as Stripe or M‑Pesa when billing is live, and secure cloud hosting providers. We share only what's strictly needed and we hold these partners to data‑protection terms.

8. How long we keep things

We keep your account and active‑automation data while you use the service. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where we're legally required to keep something (for example, billing records). The moment you disconnect a TikTok post, we stop reading it and purge that data on a short schedule.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time — just email us and a human will handle it. Where laws like the GDPR or the Kenya Data Protection Act apply, we honour the rights and timelines they give you.

10. Children's privacy

JibuFlow isn't intended for children under 13 (or under 18 without a guardian where required). We don't knowingly collect data from children, and if we ever learn we have, we'll delete it promptly.

11. Where your data lives

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. Whether you're in Nairobi or anywhere else in the world, we comply with applicable laws — including the Kenya Data Protection Act and the GDPR where relevant — and we put proper safeguards in place for any cross‑border transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

When we update this policy we'll post the new date at the top, and for anything material we'll notify you in‑app or by email before it takes effect.

13. Contact us

For anything privacy‑related, write to privacy@jibuflow.ai (or legal@jibuflow.ai). Real people, real replies.

Thanks for reading — and for trusting us with your business. We'll work hard to deserve it every single day. 💜
